How it works

Built on OpenTelemetry. No proprietary agents.

Instrument once with the open standard. If you ever move, your instrumentation moves with you.

01 · instrument

Your services

Turn on eBPF tracing and every app in the cluster is traced with no code change and no restart. Or use any OpenTelemetry SDK.

Java.NETPythonNode.jsGoBrowser
02 · collect

OTel Collector

The Helm chart deploys collectors for host, kubelet and cluster metrics and container logs.

03 · analyse

NebuLens

One OTLP endpoint receives everything: http://nebulens:4318. Summaries are pre-computed every minute, so pages stay fast.

04 · store

Your database

SQLite on a local disk for a single node, or a Postgres database of its own (not your application's). Retention is yours to set.

Measured, not promised

Fast at millions of spans.

Page load times before and after the rollup work in v10.11–v10.16, measured on AWS EKS with 3.1 million spans stored. One instance ingests about 1,500–2,500 spans per second: plenty for clusters up to about 25–50 nodes, not for very high volume.

Your telemetry. Your servers. Your rules.

NebuLens runs inside your network. Nothing is sent to us, and every release is scanned before it ships.
