Questions

What counts as a node?

One Kubernetes node or one virtual machine whose workloads send data to NebuLens. On Kubernetes, Settings → Plan counts the nodes that reported in the last 24 hours; for VMs you tell us how many you run.

What happens if I go over my node count?

A banner tells you, and nothing else changes. NebuLens never refuses or deletes your data because of the licence.

Does the licence phone home?

No. Keys are signed and checked offline inside your install, so NebuLens works in air-gapped networks and never reports usage.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

NebuLens keeps running as Community. Nothing is deleted; limits above Community stop applying to new items until you add a licence.

Do you charge for data volume or users?

No. Pro and Enterprise are priced by node only. Send as many traces, logs and metrics as your hardware can store, with as many users as you like.

How do I buy or ask a question?

Email sales@nebulens.dev with your company, node count and needs; you get a quote and a licence key, usually within one business day. Prefer GitHub? Open a request there (public).

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Pro is $129 a month billed monthly (5 nodes included, then $15 per node), or $99 a month billed annually (then $12 per node). Cancel a monthly plan any time; the licence runs to the end of the month you paid for.

Are there discounts?

Startups (under 3 years old, under 30 people) get 50% off the first year. Our first 10 design partners get 40% off for two years in return for feedback and a case study.
