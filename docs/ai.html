Bring your own model

Works with the AI you already trust.

Use Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI, or any service with an OpenAI-compatible API, including models you host inside your network. No key at all? NebuLens falls back to its built-in rule-based analysis.

Private by design

Your data, your model, your rules.

You choose where it goes

Questions and the query results needed to answer them go only to the provider you configure. Point it at a model inside your network and nothing leaves at all.

Sensitive values masked first

Card numbers, keys, tokens and other sensitive values are redacted when telemetry arrives, before the assistant can ever see them.

Off means off

Leave AI unconfigured and NebuLens makes no AI calls. Live Insights and the built-in investigation still find anomalies for you.
